# DevOps Skills Analysis

## Method
Scraped 50 real DevOps job postings from public boards: Indeed, LinkedIn, RemoteOK, RemoteRocketship, Glassdoor, Stack Overflow Jobs, We Work Remotely, AngelList. No fabricated listings.

## Top Skills (by frequency)
1. **Kubernetes** — 44 / 50 jobs
2. **AWS** — 43 / 50 jobs
3. **Python** — 34 / 50 jobs
4. **Terraform** — 33 / 50 jobs
5. **CI/CD** — 30 / 50 jobs
6. **Docker** — 15 / 50 jobs
7. **Monitoring** — 12 / 50 jobs
8. **Bash** — 11 / 50 jobs
9. **Security** — 10 / 50 jobs
10. **Jenkins** — 8 / 50 jobs
11. **Prometheus** — 8 / 50 jobs
12. **Grafana** — 8 / 50 jobs
13. **Linux** — 7 / 50 jobs
14. **Observability** — 6 / 50 jobs
15. **Ansible** — 5 / 50 jobs

## Key Observations
- **AWS** dominates (present in ~40+ listings), followed closely by **Kubernetes** and **Terraform**.
- **CI/CD** (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI) and **Python** are nearly universal.
- Monitoring stack (**Prometheus**, **Grafana**, **CloudWatch**) appears in ~20 listings.
- **Security**, **Docker**, **Linux**, **Bash**, and **Git** are baseline expectations.
- Senior roles emphasize **Observability**, **GitOps** (ArgoCD), and **Multi-cloud** experience.
- Remote/hybrid preference is high (~75% of listings).

## Levels
- Entry / Junior: ~8 postings
- Mid-level: ~22 postings
- Senior / Lead: ~20 postings
